# Instagram Outreach + WhatsApp Notification Automation

Build a fully automated lead generation system for my Virtual Assistant service.

## Objective

Every day at 9:00 AM, the system should automatically find 5 new e-commerce Instagram accounts, send them an outreach message, and notify me via WhatsApp after the messages are sent.

If any Instagram account replies to our outreach message, the system should automatically send a short acknowledgment message and immediately notify me via WhatsApp so I can manually continue the conversation.

---

## Daily Outreach Workflow

### Step 1: Find Leads

Every day at exactly 9:00 AM:

* Find 5 new e-commerce Instagram business accounts.
* Do not select accounts that were contacted previously.
* Store contacted accounts in a database.
* Save:

  * Instagram Username
  * Profile URL
  * Date Contacted
  * Message Status

---

### Step 2: Send Outreach Message

Send the following Instagram DM to all 5 selected accounts:

Hi 👋

We came across your page and loved what you're building 🚀

We provide a Virtual Assistant 🤖 that can take care of repetitive business tasks automatically.

Tell us your biggest business challenge 💭, and we'll create a solution tailored to your needs ⚡

We also share business growth ideas 📈 and marketing strategies 🎯 if you're interested.

---

### Step 3: WhatsApp Notification After Outreach

After all 5 messages have been successfully sent, send me a WhatsApp notification.

Notification Format:

✅ Daily Instagram Outreach Completed

📅 Date: DD/MM/YYYY
⏰ Time: 09:00 AM

Today's Outreach Accounts:

1. @username1
2. @username2
3. @username3
4. @username4
5. @username5

📊 Total Messages Sent: 5

All outreach messages have been sent successfully.

---

## Reply Handling Workflow

Monitor Instagram DMs continuously.

Whenever any lead replies to our outreach message:

### Step 1: Auto Reply

Automatically send:

Thank you for your response 🙌

We appreciate your interest.

Our team will review your message and DM you soon today 🚀

### Step 2: WhatsApp Notification

Immediately send a WhatsApp notification containing:

* Instagram Username
* Instagram Profile Link
* Customer Reply Message
* Date and Time

Example:

🚨 New Lead Reply Received

👤 Instagram: @store_name

💬 Customer Message:
"Interested. Can you tell me more?"

🔗 Profile:
[https://instagram.com/store_name](https://instagram.com/store_name)

⏰ Time:
10:45 AM

Action Required:
Please continue the conversation manually on Instagram.

---

## Important Rules

* Run automatically every day at 9:00 AM.
* Contact only e-commerce Instagram accounts.
* Never message the same account twice.
* Maintain a database of all contacted accounts.
* Send only 5 outreach messages per day.
* After sending messages, notify me through WhatsApp.
* If a lead replies, send the acknowledgment message automatically.
* Immediately notify me through WhatsApp with the Instagram account name and customer reply.
* I will manually continue the conversation after receiving the WhatsApp notification.
* No chatbot conversations beyond the acknowledgment message.
* No CRM.
* No dashboard required.

---

## Suggested Tech Stack

* Instagram Messaging Integration
* WhatsApp Business API or Twilio WhatsApp
* Google Sheets or Airtable for storing contacted accounts
* Scheduler/Cron Trigger for daily 9:00 AM execution

The final automation should work completely hands-free, only notifying me via WhatsApp after outreach is completed and whenever a lead responds.
